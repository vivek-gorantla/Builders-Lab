# Submission Guide - Project 01: Inventory Manager

Before submitting your solution, verify the following checklist:

- [ ] `InventoryManager` class is implemented in `solution.py`.
- [ ] Product IDs are validated for uniqueness.
- [ ] Non-negative price and quantity constraints are enforced.
- [ ] Stock increments and decrements correctly update product quantities.
- [ ] Insufficient stock operations raise a `ValueError`.
- [ ] Accessing missing products raises a `KeyError`.
- [ ] All `pytest` unit tests pass cleanly.

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Functional Correctness**: 50 points
- **Edge Case Handling**: 20 points
- **Error Handling & Validation**: 10 points
- **Code Quality & Clean Architecture**: 10 points
- **Performance & Efficiency**: 10 points

**Total**: 100 points
